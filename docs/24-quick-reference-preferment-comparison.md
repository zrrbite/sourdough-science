# Quick Reference: Preferment Comparison

| Property | Biga | Poolish | Stiff Levain | Liquid Levain | Hybrid (Yeast + Levain) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hydration | 50–60% | 100% | 50–60% | 100% | Any (levain separate) |
| Microbiology | S. cerevisiae | S. cerevisiae | Wild yeast + LAB | Wild yeast + LAB | S. cerevisiae + LAB |
| Primary Acid | Minimal | Minimal | Acetic (sharp) | Lactic (mild) | Tuneable (levain type) |
| pH Range | 5.5–6.0 | 5.5–6.0 | 3.5–4.5 | 3.8–4.5 | 4.5–5.5 |
| Flavour Character | Yeast aromatics | Sweet, wheaty | Sharp, vinegary | Round, creamy | Complex esters, balanced tang |
| Gluten Effect | Elastic, tight | Extensible, soft | Extensible + protease | Extensible + protease | Mostly elastic, slight acid softening |
| Crumb Structure | Fine, uniform | Open, regular | Wild, irregular | Open, moderate | Controlled open, uniform |
| Ferment Time | 12–24h | 8–16h | 6–12h (cycle) | 4–8h (cycle) | 2–3h bulk + cold retard |
| Best For | Ciabatta, focaccia | Baguettes, Neapolitan pizza | Country bread | Enriched doughs | Home oven pizza |
| Pizza Suitability | Good | Excellent (traditional) | Viable | Good | Optimal for home oven |

*Same vessel, same hydration, completely different ecology. Or: combine them.*
