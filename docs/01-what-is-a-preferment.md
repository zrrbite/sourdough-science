# What Is a Preferment?

A preferment is a portion of dough that is fermented in advance of the final mix. The purpose is threefold: to develop flavour complexity through extended fermentation, to modify gluten structure before the final dough is assembled, and to give the baker a head start on gas production. All preferments share this fundamental role, but they diverge sharply in their microbiology, hydration, and the resulting impact on dough behaviour and final product.
