# Sourdough starter: 14-day cheat sheet

For the kitchen; print [starter-cheatsheet.pdf](starter-cheatsheet.pdf) (one A4 page). The
method's source, what each stage is, and the literature are in [Starting a Starter From
Scratch](starter-from-scratch.md).

**You need:** a flour mix of 350 g bread flour + 150 g dark rye (30% rye), a straight-sided
jar with a rubber band, a 0.1 g scale and 24°C. Cloth cover until day 8, then a loose lid.

## Schedule — grams of starter / water / flour

| Day | Morning | Evening | What you'll see |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | — | 0 / 30 / 30 | Mix only, rest 24 h |
| 2 | — | Stir, clean jar | Dry skin on top: fine |
| 3 | — | 20 / 20 / 20 | Risen: bacteria, not yeast yet |
| 4 | 20 / 20 / 20 | 16 / 16 / 16 | 3–4×, runny, cheesy smell |
| 5 | 15 / 15 / 15 | 16 / 16 / 16 | **Almost no rise, vomit smell: normal** |
| 6–7 | 15 / 15 / 15 | 15 / 15 / 15 | 1.2–2×, milder, first sour notes |
| 8 | 15 / 15 / 15 | 15 / 15 / 15 | ~2×. Save discard, loose lid |
| 9 | 15 / 15 / 15 | 8 / 16 / 16 | 2.5× |
| 10 | 8 / 16 / 16 | 5 / 15 / 15 | 2.5×, falls before the feed |
| 11 | 5 / 20 / 20 | 5 / 25 / 25 | 2.5× |
| 12 | 5 / 25 / 25 | 5 / 20 / 20 | Evening: switch to 10% rye mix |
| 13 | 5 / 25 / 25 | 5 / 25 / 25 | 2.5× |
| 14 | 8 / 16 / 16 ×2 | 10 / 50 / 50 | Feeds 7 h apart; three a day now |

## Rules

- **Feed at peak:** fallen before the feed? Next feed one ratio up, 1:2:2 → 1:3:3 → 1:4:4 → 1:5:5.
- **Day 12:** 10% rye mix = leftover 30% mix + twice its weight in bread flour.
- **Done** when it triples in 4–6 h on 1:2:2 at 27°C (oven, light on). Video's day 14: 3× in 7 h.
- **Discard** if pink, orange or fuzzy. **No 40°C+ builds** until done. Fridge: 2–4 weeks.
- **Dried backup:** soak 10 g in 25 g water at 35–38°C, 30 min; +20 g flour; then as day 4.

Source: [Full Proof Baking, 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m6pGkOuZnrk)
